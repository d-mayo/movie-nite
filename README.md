<h1 align="center">Movie Nite</h1>

<p align="center">Pick tonight's film for the FFF movie-night group: viewers nominate films from TMDB, and a weighted, editable wheel picks what to watch.</p>

## Sounds

The projector sounds on the wheel's spin are cut from two CC0 recordings by Stefan021 on Freesound: [overhead projector](https://freesound.org/people/Stefan021/sounds/702451/) and [8 mm projector, long run with finish](https://freesound.org/people/Stefan021/sounds/412145/). The cut points are in [src/assets/sound/README.md](src/assets/sound/README.md).
